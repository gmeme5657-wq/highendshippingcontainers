Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and only collect the information needed to provide container quotes, process orders, manage deliveries, and support customers.

Collected data may include contact details, order details, delivery preferences, and anonymized website analytics. We never sell your personal information to unrelated third parties.

Shipping Policy

Highend Shipping Containers delivers throughout Australia with secure transport, site access planning, and damage inspection on arrival.

Shipping timelines vary by location, product type, and permit requirements. We work with reliable carriers for on-time delivery and coordinate with your site for safe unloading.

Returns & Refunds

Our returns process is designed for transparency. If a product is damaged on arrival or differs materially from the description, contact us immediately.

Refunds are assessed case-by-case and depend on inspection, restocking, and return shipping arrangements. Delivery fees may not be refundable.
