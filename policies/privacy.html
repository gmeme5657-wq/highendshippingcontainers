Privacy Policy

How Highend Shipping Containers collects, uses, protects, and manages customer information across inquiries, orders, deliveries, and support.

Last updated: January 2026

Overview

Highend Shipping Containers respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you browse our website, request a quote, place an order, contact support, or subscribe to updates.

This page is written to be easy to understand. It does not limit any privacy rights you may have under applicable law.

Information We Collect

We collect information needed to operate an ecommerce container business and coordinate large-item delivery.

  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number, company name, and billing or delivery address.
  • Order and quote information such as product selections, quantities, delivery requirements, site access notes, and payment status.
  • Technical data such as browser type, IP address, approximate location, device type, pages viewed, and referral source.
  • Communication records from email, forms, phone calls, support requests, and follow-up messages.
  • Payment information processed through secure third-party payment providers. We do not intentionally store full card numbers on our website.

How We Use Information

We use customer information for practical business purposes, including:

  • Responding to quote requests, product questions, and service inquiries.
  • Processing orders, payments, confirmations, delivery planning, and support.
  • Coordinating freight, site access, delivery appointments, and damage claims.
  • Improving our website, product catalog, search, security, and customer experience.
  • Sending service-related messages and, when permitted, promotional updates.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, unauthorized activity, and technical issues.

When We Share Information

We only share information when it helps us provide the service, comply with law, or protect our business and customers.

  • Payment processors for secure transaction handling.
  • Freight carriers, delivery partners, and logistics providers for shipping coordination.
  • Technology providers that host, secure, analyze, or maintain our website and systems.
  • Professional advisors, insurers, or legal authorities when required for compliance, claims, or lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not trade customer contact details to unrelated third parties for their independent marketing.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure traffic, improve product discovery, and understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but some site functions may not work correctly if cookies are disabled.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for orders, legal records, tax obligations, customer service, fraud prevention, and business operations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of record and applicable requirements.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect customer information. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to reduce risk and protect your information responsibly.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of certain personal information where applicable. You can also unsubscribe from marketing emails using the instructions in those messages or by contacting us.
